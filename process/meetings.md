# Template for meetings

- Goals: What's the purpose of the meeting and expected outcomes
- Scope: what's in scope and what's explicitly out of scope
- Participants: Who are the required decision makers and experts. (Everybody else is optional)
  - Empower attendees to _decline_ or _exit_ a meeting if they believe they cannot contribute or their presense is not necessary
- Pre-reading materials, preparation expected from participants
- Agenda: Discussion points and allocated time slots for each. 
  - Consider allocating time at the beginning of the meeting for reading material
  - Allocate time at the end to summarise decisions

Note: Always start and end a meeting on designated time. When this is the norm, it keeps the discussions focussed.
